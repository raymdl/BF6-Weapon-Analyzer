Distance Spray Preview

Projects the existing recoil and spread simulation from angular degrees onto a flat wall at selected distances. The dot positions are still produced by the same recoil and spread model; only the display unit changes to meters on the wall.

Projection uses tan(angle) * distance for both recoil and spread offsets. Spread landing uses uniform-over-radius sampling, matching the current app behavior. The target outline and zone boundaries are illustrative and are not datamined collision geometry.
